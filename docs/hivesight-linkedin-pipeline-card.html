What makes it trustworthy

Data governance

Source rights, attribution, contribution permission, withdrawal, and quarantine.

Human review

Model suggestions become evidence only after reviewed annotation decisions.

Benchmarking

Protected benchmark sets measure each model purpose separately and end to end.

Statistical layer

Detection output is evidence for an estimate, not the estimate itself.

Design stance

✓Separate bee localisation, orientation, and mite detection.
✓Report uncertainty and coverage, not just a tidy number.
✓Keep consent, provenance, and evaluation visible.
